### 1. What this covers

This Agreement covers the design, build, hosting, and ongoing marketing care of one website for your business, as described below.

### 2. The Build — $[1,000–1,500] flat, one time

- **Scope:** the site shown to you as a free mockup, built out fully — up to [ __ ] pages, a working contact form, and mobile-friendly design. Includes up to two rounds of revisions after the mockup; anything beyond that is scoped and quoted separately.
- **Payment:** $[ ___ ], due in full before the site goes live. No partial builds — the price reflects that we've already shown you the real thing before you pay anything.
- **Timeline:** we'll give you an estimated delivery date once we have what we need from you (Section 3). Delays on your end (photos, text, logins) push the timeline back.

### 3. What we need from you

Business details, photos, written content, and your logo if you have one. The site can't go live without these — the sooner they're in, the sooner the site is.

### 4. Ownership

Clementine owns the design and source code of the site unless and until you complete the Buyout (Section 8). Paying the Build fee (Section 2) gives you the right to use the live, hosted site — not ownership of the underlying code or design — for as long as you're either current on the Pro Care Plan (Section 5) or the site is in the frozen state described in Section 6. Completing the Buyout transfers full ownership of the source code and design to you.

### 5. Pro Care Plan — $[ ___ ]/month, optional

You can choose to add ongoing hosting, support, and marketing management for $[ ___ ]/month, billed automatically to the card or bank account on file. It covers everything in Clementine's standard Care Plan, plus active, ongoing marketing work:

- **Hosting** — the site stays live and online.
- **Unlimited small edits** — text changes, photo swaps, hours, pricing, promos — typically turned around within 2 business days, priority turnaround over standard Care Plan clients.
- **One full redesign, included** — whenever you want it, no extra charge. We'll agree on a timeline together when you ask for it.
- **Analytics and call tracking** — Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Tag Manager installed and monitored, so there's a real record of site traffic and contact-form/call activity over time.
- **Local business schema markup** — structured data (business name, address, phone, hours, service area) kept current so search engines can read your business info accurately.
- **Google Business Profile management** — setup or cleanup of your Google Business Profile, plus an ongoing system for requesting reviews from your customers/patients.
- **Technical health checks** — periodic checks on page load speed and basic site health, with fixes made as needed rather than left to accumulate.
- **A monthly written check-in** — a short note on what changed and what's next, sent once a month.
- **Bigger requests** (online ordering, booking systems, new sections) are scoped and quoted separately, on a case-by-case basis.

No contract, no minimum term — cancel anytime.

**What we don't guarantee under this plan:** we manage the technical and structural marketing elements above in good faith, but we don't guarantee specific search rankings, traffic volumes, or lead/patient counts — see Section 9.

### 6. If you cancel the Pro Care Plan

Canceling doesn't take your site down. It freezes exactly as it is — live, online, but no further edits — until you do one of the following:

- **(a)** Pay a flat **$[ ___ ]** for a new, unscoped round of work (treated like a fresh build); the site freezes again afterward unless you resubscribe, or
- **(b)** Resubscribe to the Pro Care Plan, or
- **(c)** Complete the Buyout (Section 8) and take the site elsewhere.

### 7. Domain name

- **Already have a domain?** You keep managing and owning it — nothing changes.
- **Need one registered?** We'll register it with **you** listed as the owner of record; we just manage the day-to-day technical setup.
- The first year of domain registration is included in the Build fee. Ongoing renewal (roughly $12–15/year) is included while you're on the Pro Care Plan. If you're not on the plan, we'll reach out near the renewal date to offer to renew it for the fee or hand the account fully over to you; if we don't hear back, it lapses through the registrar in the normal course — we won't renew it out of pocket on your behalf.

### 8. Buyout — $[ ___ ] flat, anytime

At any point, you can pay a flat $[ ___ ] to take full ownership of the site's source code and design and host it independently, wherever you like. This ends any Pro Care Plan billing and our hosting/marketing-management responsibility for the site.

### 9. What we don't guarantee

We build, host, and manage marketing elements in good faith, but we don't guarantee specific business outcomes (traffic, leads, sales, search rankings), and we're not responsible for outages or ranking changes caused by third-party services outside our control (hosting providers, domain registrars, payment processors, search engines, etc.).

### 10. Liability

Our total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amount you've paid us in the 12 months before a claim. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential damages.

- This is the whole agreement between us about the site — any changes need to be in writing and agreed to by both of us.
- If any part of this Agreement turns out to be unenforceable, the rest still stands.
- We may update our standard terms for new clients over time, but changes won't apply to you retroactively without your written consent.
